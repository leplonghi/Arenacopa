# ArenaCopa Cinematic Splash Design

## Style Prompt

Create a cinematic football-app splash screen that feels like a premium broadcast opening: deep stadium darkness, controlled gold highlights, green field energy, and a short crest reveal. The motion should be deliberate, polished, and app-native, not a promotional ad.

## Colors

- Background: `#010604` for the near-black arena base.
- Deep field green: `#02110D` for ambient depth and grass-light shadows.
- Primary gold: `#FFC212` for the ArenaCopa reveal and premium glow.
- Neon pitch green: `#7EFF48` for field lines and live energy.
- Text white: `#F2F7F1` for the brand lockup.

## Typography

- Display: `Barlow Condensed`, backed by `Teko` and `Rajdhani`, for the ArenaCopa wordmark.
- UI/body: `Manrope` for small labels and loading copy.

## Motion Rules

- Total splash duration is 3 seconds.
- Start in darkness, reveal field geometry, then reveal the mark and brand.
- Use slow cinematic scale, glow, and opacity changes.
- Respect reduced-motion users with a shorter, calmer reveal.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not make it feel like a long ad or marketing hero.
- Do not use generic blue/purple tech gradients.
- Do not add excess copy beyond the app name and one short cue.
- Do not use random particles or nondeterministic animation.
- Do not block the app after the 3-second intro completes.
